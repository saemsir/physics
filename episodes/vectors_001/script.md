# Breaking a vector into components

- **Audience:** NEET/JEE students reviewing vectors; suitable as a general
  physics refresher.
- **Target length:** 30-60 seconds.
- **Learning outcome:** Read the horizontal and vertical components of a
  vector from a diagram and express the vector using unit vectors.
- **Prerequisites:** Cartesian axes and the meaning of a directed vector.

## Core idea

A vector's horizontal and vertical parts add tip-to-tail to make the original
vector.

## Narration and visual beats

| Beat | Visual on screen | Narration / explanation | Equation or label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | Show a diagonal arrow from the origin to a point above and to the right. | "A diagonal vector can look like one complicated motion. But on a grid, we can describe it using two simpler moves." | Label the arrow `A`. |
| Horizontal part | Draw the horizontal arrow from the origin to the point directly below the tip. | "First, move three diagram units in the positive x direction. This is the x-component." | Label the arrow `A_x=3`. |
| Vertical part | From the horizontal arrow's tip, draw upward to the original tip. | "Then move two diagram units in the positive y direction. This is the y-component." | Label the arrow `A_y=2`. |
| Combine | Hold the three arrows on screen; emphasize the diagonal arrow. | "Put those moves tip-to-tail and you reach the original endpoint. The vector is three i-hat plus two j-hat." | `\vec A = 3\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath}` |
| Recap | Keep the component arrows and diagonal visible together. | "Add vector components with their directions. Do not add their lengths as ordinary numbers: the diagonal magnitude here is the square root of thirteen, not five." | `|\vec A|=\sqrt{3^2+2^2}=\sqrt{13}` |

## Accuracy notes

- The Manim scene draws a vector from `(0, 0)` to `(3, 2)` and shows positive
  x and y components in schematic diagram units.
- Components are signed; magnitudes of vectors are non-negative.
