## Accuracy and design review

- [ ] The visual distinguishes the object from its representation.
- [ ] The transformation is defined, not presented as a black box.
- [ ] Initial/boundary terms and conditions are shown where they occur.
- [ ] The operation is simpler for a stated reason, not merely moved off-screen.
- [ ] The inverse step and the original problem's answer are visible.
- [ ] A direct substitution, limiting case, or other independent check is shown.
- [ ] The visual does not imply a stronger equivalence than the mathematics.
- [ ] Source, derivation, physics/math review, and preview approval are recorded.
