# SaemPhysics channel brief

## Purpose

Make physics understandable by connecting a concrete question to a clear
visual model, a careful derivation, and a result learners can use.

## Audience

- **NEET:** intuition, standard models, units, graphs, and justified
  problem-solving.
- **JEE Main/Advanced:** assumptions, derivations, limiting cases, multi-step
  reasoning, and common traps.
- **Physics Olympiads:** modeling, proof, estimation, alternate approaches, and
  extensions beyond routine syllabus patterns.

Use one primary audience and one learning outcome per episode. Adapt depth
through companion segments rather than trying to teach every level at once.

## Editorial format

- **Central question:** one genuine question or puzzle per episode.
- **Narrative arc:** hook, intuition, representation, formal model, derivation,
  test or counterexample, exam connection, recap—as appropriate to the topic.
- **First milestone:** one correct, understandable 30-60 second scene.
- **Language:** choose and record the primary narration language per episode;
  the channel-level language is still to be confirmed.
- **Visual identity:** use the original color and typography tokens in
  `config/brand.py`. Motion should reveal a physical or mathematical
  relationship, not decorate it.
- **Academic standard:** ground lessons in Feynman, MIT-level professor
  lectures and materials, and established books; use 3Blue1Brown as a
  mathematical-visualization reference while checking claims against scholarly
  sources. See [`visual_teaching_principles.md`](visual_teaching_principles.md).

## What makes an episode SaemPhysics

1. It explains the physical idea before asking students to memorize a formula.
2. It defines symbols and makes assumptions, signs, units, and limits visible.
3. Each animation encodes a meaningful quantity or transformation.
4. It returns to the opening question and leaves the learner with a checkable
   understanding.
5. Physics, narration, and final visuals receive teacher approval.

Take inspiration from broad teaching methods, not another creator's scripts,
artwork, branding, or distinctive scenes.
